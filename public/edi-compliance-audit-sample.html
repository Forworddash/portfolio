Executive summary

Over the 90 days reviewed, Northline incurred $61,240 in Walmart deductions. $28,385 of that — 46% — traces to four correctable EDI defects, not to fulfillment or carrier performance. Annualized, those defects represent $113,540 in recoverable cost. None require a platform change, and the largest single item is a scheduling fix.

$113,540 Annualized recoverable cost from four correctable defects

Northline's OTIF score for the period was 97.2%, below Walmart's 98% threshold. A substantial share of that shortfall is not caused by late trucks. It is caused by advance ship notices arriving after the goods they describe — which scores identically to a late delivery and is invisible in the deduction coding.

What I'd do first

Fix Finding 1. It is the largest item by a wide margin, the effort is roughly one day of work at the integration layer, and it requires no mapping changes. Finding 5 should be actioned in the same window — it is unquantified only because the necessary evidence isn't currently being retained, which is itself the problem.

The findings in full

01 ASN transmitted after DC receipt on cross-dock lanes
High severity $71,200 / yr Low effort Owner: EDI vendor

What's happening

On three cross-dock lanes, the 856 is generated by a nightly batch at 23:00. Those shipments reach the DC in under six hours, so the ASN routinely arrives after the goods do.

Evidence

212 of 1,208 ASNs transmitted after DC receipt; 189 of those on lanes 4412, 4418 and 4501. Matching deductions coded to delivery compliance total $17,800 over the period. Sampled ASN 0004412: shipment departed 14:20, ASN transmitted 23:04, DC receipt logged 19:47 — the ASN arrived more than three hours after the freight.

Root cause

ASN generation runs on a fixed nightly schedule rather than firing on ship-confirm. That works for two-day lanes and cannot work for cross-dock, where transit time is shorter than the gap to the next batch window. Nothing is wrong with the map.

Business impact

$17,800 per quarter, roughly $71,200 annually. These deductions present as delivery failures and are therefore being escalated to the carrier and the 3PL, where no fix is possible. Every one of these trucks was on time.

Fix

Trigger the 856 on ship-confirm for cross-dock lanes rather than on the nightly batch. No mapping change is required — this is scheduling only. If the batch cannot be made event-driven, an interim fix is a second intra-day run at 15:00 covering cross-dock lanes, which addresses most of the exposure.

02 ASN case counts sourced from pick list, not packed quantity
High severity $18,440 / yr Medium effort Owner: Warehouse systems

What's happening

ASNs report the quantity that was supposed to ship rather than what physically shipped. Whenever a short-pick occurs, the ASN overstates the shipment and Walmart's receiving scan disagrees with it.

Evidence

143 of 1,208 ASNs showed a quantity variance at receipt. Corresponding deductions at $0.25 per case over/under total $4,610 for the period. Sampled ASN 0004488 declared 48 cases against PO 8842119; the receiving report shows 44. The pick ticket records a 4-case short-pick that the ASN never reflected.

Root cause

The ASN is generated at pick-release rather than pack-confirm. SN1 element 02 is populated from the ordered quantity on the inbound 850 rather than from the confirmed packed quantity in the WMS. Nothing re-reads the shipment after packing, so any variance introduced during picking is invisible to the ASN.

Business impact

Approximately $18,440 annually in direct deductions. The larger cost is indirect: quantity variances degrade the in-full component of OTIF, contributing to the 97.2% score and exposure to the 3%-of-COGS penalty on non-compliant shipments.

Fix

Move ASN generation to fire on pack-confirm and source SN1 02 from the WMS confirmed quantity. If the ERP cannot defer generation, the interim control is a pre-transmission check that blocks any ASN whose SN1 totals do not reconcile to the shipment record.

03 GTINs not updated after packaging change on three SKUs
Medium severity $14,300 / yr Low effort Owner: Master data

What's happening

Three SKUs moved from 12-count to 6-count inner packs in March. Walmart's item file and the ERP item master were both updated. The EDI translator's item cross-reference was not, so outbound documents still carry the retired GTINs.

Evidence

84 ASNs and 82 invoices in the period carried retired GTINs for SKUs NH-2201, NH-2204 and NH-2209. Deductions coded to item/UPC discrepancy total $3,575.

Root cause

The translator maintains its own item cross-reference table, updated manually and independently of the ERP item master. The March packaging change followed a checklist that does not include the translator table.

Fix

Point the translator's cross-reference at the ERP item master so there is a single source of truth. Where that isn't feasible, add the translator table to the packaging-change checklist and add a pre-transmission check that rejects any GTIN absent from the current retailer item file. Without this, the problem recurs with every future packaging change.

04 Invoice line references don't match the originating PO
Medium severity $9,600 / yr Medium effort Owner: EDI vendor

What's happening

810 invoices renumber their lines sequentially instead of carrying the line reference from the originating 850. On a fully shipped order the numbering coincidentally aligns; on a partial shipment it does not, and Walmart cannot reconcile line-for-line.

Evidence

61 invoices in the period showed a line mismatch against the originating PO, all of them on partially shipped orders. Deductions coded to invoice discrepancy total $2,400.

Root cause

IT1 element 01 is populated with a generated sequence number rather than being mapped from PO1 element 01 of the inbound 850. The translator does not retain the PO line reference through to invoicing.

Fix

Persist the PO1 01 value from the 850 through the order record and map it to IT1 01 at invoice generation. Note that the AR time spent researching and disputing these exceeds the deduction value itself.

05 4% of outbound documents never acknowledged
High severity Unquantified Low effort Owner: VAN / EDI vendor

What's happening

52 of 1,208 ASNs and 41 of 1,190 invoices have no corresponding 997 functional acknowledgment. Nothing in the current configuration flags this, so no one is aware of it.

Evidence

Reconciliation of the outbound log against received 997s. The gaps cluster around 18–21 May and 9 June, which suggests episodes rather than a steady rate.

Root cause

Cannot be determined from the data supplied. Either the documents never left the VAN, or the acknowledgments were received and not retained. Both are serious and they require different remedies, so this needs to be established before anything is changed.

Business impact

Unquantified, and potentially the largest item in this report. An unacknowledged ASN is one Walmart may never have received. The resulting deduction arrives as a missing-ASN penalty with no evidence trail available to dispute it — so these are both expensive and unrecoverable after the fact.

Fix

Enable 997 reconciliation alerting at the translator: any outbound document without a matching acknowledgment within 24 hours raises an exception. Most platforms support this and ship with it disabled. Then retain 997s for a rolling 24 months so the evidence exists next time a deduction is disputed. This is configuration, not development — the highest ratio of risk removed to work required in this report.
